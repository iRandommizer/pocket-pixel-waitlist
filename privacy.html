POCKET PIXEL WAITLIST · LAST UPDATED 2026-09-28

This page explains what happens to your information when you join the Pocket Pixel waitlist. Pocket Pixel is made by one person (Yuan, @chiusday.art), not a company.

WHAT'S COLLECTED

  • Your email address (required)
  • Your name and Instagram handle, if you choose to give them
  • Your answers to the optional "what would you put on yours" questions and any notes you write
  • Which language you're browsing in

No cookies, analytics, ad trackers, or session recording run on this site.

WHY

Solely to hold your place on the waitlist and to email you when the next batch is ready. Your name, if given, personalizes that email.

WHERE IT GOES

Your submission passes through a Cloudflare Worker (which only relays the request) into a Google Sheet that only Yuan can access. It isn't sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything beyond this waitlist.

HOW LONG IT'S KEPT

Until the batch you're waiting for ships, or until you ask for it to be deleted — whichever comes first.

YOUR RIGHTS

Email yuantchiu@gmail.com any time to see, correct, or delete what's on file for you, or to be taken off the list.
